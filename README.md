# 🍔 Streat Eats Landing Page & Blog Hub

> **Official Landing Page and Multi-Page SEO Blog Platform for Streat Eats — Haldwani's Hyperlocal Street Food Delivery Startup.**

Founded by **Karan Kumar** (Founder & CEO) and **Lokesh Paneru** (Co-Founder & Head of Operations).

---

## 🚀 Website Highlights
- **100% Zero Food Markup:** Exact stall prices for Haldwani's best street foods (Momos, Burgers, Chaat, Rolls).
- **Fast 30-Minute Delivery:** Hyperlocal rider network across Bhotia Parao, Nagar Palika, Mukhani, and Tikonia.
- **21+ SEO-Optimized Articles:** Comprehensive local food guides and founder stories written in English and Hindi.
- **Full Schema.org Knowledge Graph:** Pre-configured JSON-LD structured data for Google Search rich results.

---

## 📁 Repository Structure
- `index.html` — Main Homepage & Landing Page
- `blogs.html` / `blog.html` — Blog Hub with category filters (Founders, Food Guides, Hindi, Tech)
- `blog-*.html` — 21 dedicated, standalone SEO blog detail pages
- `blog.css` — Unified responsive stylesheet for all blog pages
- `sitemap.xml` & `robots.txt` — Search engine sitemap and crawl rules
- `*.jpg`, `*.webp`, `*.png` — High-resolution optimized assets and founder photos
- `generate_all_20_blogs.js` & `blog_templates.js` — Automated blog builder & template engine
